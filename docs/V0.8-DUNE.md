# kRAGE v0.8: four-map build

| Map | Size | Layout and identity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dune | 72 × 60 m | Desert pumping settlement; market, covered service passage, two terraces and a two-metre pipeline jump. Regional plaster colours, repair panels, painted wall bases, flagstone paving, dunes and a village/water-tower skyline. |
| Snow | 64 × 72 m | Alpine relay station; four-entry turbine hall, upper gallery with two stair routes, freight apron, covered service route and rocky western flank. Cold steel, orange cargo, snowy ridgelines, pines and a relay dish. |
| CELL I | 60 × 50 m | Original compact Dune layout preserved, enclosed under a solid ceiling. Teal/slate palette and ceiling lighting; no sky. |
| CELL II | 32 × 26 m | Smaller Snow training successor, roughly 59% less floor area than the former Snow arena. Raised central platform, short flank routes and crouch passage; plum/slate palette and solid ceiling. Intended for 1v1/2v2. |

Map ids remain Dune 0, Snow 1, CELL I 2; CELL II is 3. Practice, saved map choice, room creation, Quick Play, radar and shared server collision use these ids. Both services require protocol 12; reload old tabs after deploying both. No deployment was performed by this task.

Dune's projecting valve, pipe body, canopy edges, ledges, doorway frames and supports now have collision. Valve collision preserves the central opening. A movement bug in overlapping collider resolution was also fixed: collision now retains the incoming travel direction after contact zeroes velocity, preventing a second collider from ejecting the player through the prop.

Scenery is batched by material. Dune uses seven 128 × 128 procedural surface textures; facility maps use three. No large texture downloads, dynamic snowfall or volumetric lighting are required. Snow's mountains and Dune's wider landscape remain outside the playable boundaries. Detail proxies and indoor ceilings are omitted from radar fill so routes remain readable.

Design references: [Valve's Nuke redesign](https://www.counter-strike.net/reintroducing_nuke) for readable industrial spaces and alternate routes; [Riot's Icebox revision](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/deep-dive-into-icebox-4-04-changes/) for simplifying awkward angles and widening approaches. Layouts here are original, adapted to frag-based matches with few players.

Validation covers clear spawns and connected routes across all four arenas, bot combat in all modes, Snow's two stairs, Dune's pipeline jump and protrusions, enclosed ceiling shot collision, rendering budgets, room selection and existing combat/network regressions. Legacy geometry remains only as explicit fixtures for unrelated regression tests. Headless visual checks are not low-end hardware performance benchmarks.

Next: real-player testing of spawn pressure, route choices, sightlines and encounter frequency across 1v1, 2v2, 3v3 and FFA. Tune measured problems before adding maps or declaring v1 production readiness.

Final local verification: 123 tests passed (57 core, 37 presentation, 29 multiplayer), lint and TypeScript passed, Render production build passed, frontend startup returned v0.8 with four arena choices, and room health reported protocol 12. Static browser renders were inspected for all four maps. Real-player network and balance testing remains the next gate.
