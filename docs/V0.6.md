# kRAGE v0.6.0

## Implemented
- Transient transport recovery keeps the game scene open; manual retry retains the room/token and score. Late callbacks from superseded sockets are ignored. Disconnected slots remain reserved for 120 seconds.
- Stale input bursts are retired instead of throwing a fatal backlog error. A bounded token bucket tolerates delayed packet batches while retaining a sustained rate limit.
- Congested links skip obsolete snapshots and preserve the event cursor until a snapshot is sent. Remote interpolation target is 75ms. Duplicate snapshot-driven HUD updates are removed; the regular 10Hz HUD remains.
- Match duration is selectable in Quick Play and practice, saved with lobby preferences. Friends rules expose time, score, slots, bots, map and loadout. Native selects are replaced with keyboard-accessible segmented choices.
- Settings are divided into View, Sound, Movement and Performance. All three loadout choices fit the panel.
- Male/female models use different clothing palettes, tapered limbs/torso, fitted head shapes and gear. Shared rig and gameplay hit volumes remain intact. Weapons gain distinct receiver/stock/barrel details and improved SVG elevations.
- Building edges are subtly bevelled and wall/grain textures use mipmaps to reduce distant shimmer. Sand banks/snow drifts and extended terrain ground the existing arenas; combat paths/collisions are unchanged.

## Verification
104 tests passed: 49 core, 31 presentation and 24 server, including four WebSocket players and retained scores after reconnect. Lint, TypeScript, the Render production build and startup smoke passed. The smoke verifies v0.6 HTML, font delivery and protocol-9 room health on PORT-configured services. Browser checks cover compact/desktop lobby, keyboard duration selection, settings categories, loadout, map selection and practice fallback. Headless mouse capture is unavailable; drag aim is exercised instead.

Geometry inspection: Dune 9,660 triangles / 40 meshes; Snow 8,826 / 45; characters about 10.9k triangles each including their weapon. These counts are geometry checks, not hardware FPS benchmarks.

## Still required before v1
- Deploy both services with protocol 9; test the actual four friends' devices and regional hosting. Local tests do not establish internet RTT or potato-device FPS.
- Playtest deeper map redesigns: meaningful routes, cover and spawn balance require match evidence. This build polishes current arenas, not a replacement competitive map pool.
- Integrate the next user-supplied impact/kill/reload audio pack. Existing sounds remain.
- Accounts, durable progression and production operations remain deferred as requested. In-memory match recovery does not survive a server restart.
