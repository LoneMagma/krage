# kRAGE v0.7.0

## Shipped in this build
- Shared graphite, ivory and warm copper interface, custom vector wordmark and direct Play / Lobby / Locker / Challenges / Settings navigation. Compact custom room rules keep Create Lobby visible; no page scrolling is required.
- Large drag/keyboard weapon inspection with a renderer reused across weapon switches. Each primary has Factory, its existing free finish and a new earnable finish: ECHO Circuit, KILO Blackout and MICA Nightfall. Purchase and equip are separate actions; saved finishes are independent per weapon.
- Daily/weekly challenge tabs, progress, explicit claims, claim badges, wallet and reward feedback. Match results link directly to rewards. Online completion awards device-local progress using a stable per-round receipt; reconnects retain the receipt and rematches generate a new one.
- Refined chest silhouettes, variant gear, hands, premium weapon materials, two-hand lobby carry poses and restrained podium animation. Models stay procedural Three.js assets with the existing rig and combat hit volumes.
- 30 prepared audio clips (1.27 MiB): all three guns and reload phases, EDGE, impact/headshot/kill, locomotion, victory/defeat, UI and reward cues. Current defeat recording retained as requested. Supplied originals preserved; see SOUND-ASSETS.md.

## Validation
106 regression tests: 49 core, 33 presentation and 24 server. Coverage includes independent finishes and purchase ownership, reload/result sample routing, IK limb lengths, challenge claim replay protection, stable reconnect round IDs and distinct rematch IDs. Lint and TypeScript pass.

Browser checks cover 960x640 desktop and 390x844 locker layouts, full desktop lobby, buying/equipping/switching with one inspector canvas, persisted equipment, daily claims and weekly visibility, keyboard controls and custom rules. The Render production build and local startup smoke verify v0.7 HTML, audio/logo delivery, protocol 9 room health and environment-controlled ports. These are local checks, not remote latency or low-end hardware benchmarks.

## Release boundary
Deploy frontend and room service together: round receipts are an additive protocol-9 snapshot field. No deployment was performed by this build. Progress/currency remain device-local and unsuitable for real-money balances. Authentication, durable online economy and production operations stay deferred. Major asymmetrical Dune/Snow redesigns remain v0.8 work. Headphone/speaker mix playtesting and friends' device/network testing remain useful before v1.
