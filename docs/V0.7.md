# kRAGE v0.7.0

- Three new faceted Blender characters: Rook, Vera and Blake, with distinct clothing and equipment. Shared animation/ragdoll rig and hitboxes remain intact. Lobby characters stand armed without a podium and rotate by dragging or arrow keys. Editable source: `assets/blender/krage-characters.blend`; rebuild with Blender and `scripts/build-characters.py`.
- Revised KILO receiver and continuous curved magazine. New supplied logo, translucent panels, repaired practice controls, and right-footer Made in India / pacify.site credits.
- KR economy: match completion gives 20 KR, up to 40 for eliminations, up to 10 for headshots and 10 for winning. Daily/weekly challenges refresh on UTC boundaries, can be claimed once, and award an extra 50/150 KR for completing each set. Balances carry across resets. Existing balances and owned finishes are preserved.
- Each weapon has three priced finishes: Glacier/Circuit/Corona at 300/900/3600 KR; Copperhead/Blackout/Regent at 350/1050/4200; Amethyst/Nightfall/Nebula at 400/1200/4800. Prestige finishes have distinct palettes, inlays and matching SVG previews. The locker separates purchase from equip, saves each weapon independently, and shows progress toward locked finishes.
- Original v0.6 gunfire restored. Supplied reloads wired, EDGE air sound uses only the first second, slide is a soft generated swoosh, footsteps are slightly quieter. Other EDGE effects and the requested defeat clip are retained.
- Global chat plus room/team channels, mute, message length/rate limits and room/team isolation. Chat UI is available in the lobby and while paused; it does not automatically interrupt combat. Chat is live and ephemeral, without accounts or stored history.

## Verification and limits
111 tests cover gameplay, rig/mesh integrity, earned purchases, reset/claim idempotence, online recovery and chat isolation. Lint, TypeScript, production build and startup checks are run for this release. Browser checks cover lobby rotation, character switching, practice controls, locker selection and purchase persistence.

Redeploy frontend and room service together. This build remains pre-v1: KR is device-local, not a secure real-money balance; room/global chat are single-server and ephemeral. Internet latency, audio preference and low-end hardware performance still need real-device playtesting. Major competitive map redesigns remain v0.8. No deployment is performed by this build.
