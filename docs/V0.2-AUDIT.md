# krage v0.2 — handling and arena pass

## Focus

Make movement, aiming, weapon feedback and the interface agree with each other. Reduce baseline speed while preserving timed slides, air steering, jumps and fast encounters. Give the arenas distinct silhouettes and useful geometry, not just more decoration.

## Tuning

- Ground run: 6.4 m/s, down from 8.7 m/s (26% reduction).
- Ground stopping: 72 m/s². Reversal: 64 m/s². Acceleration: 46 m/s².
- Slide starts at 9.6 m/s, down from 14 m/s (31% reduction), decelerates at 7.5 m/s², lasts at most 0.48 s, and has a 1.3 s cooldown.
- Horizontal cap: 10.2 m/s. Air acceleration: 8 m/s².
- Jump: 8.4 m/s with 30 m/s² gravity. Approximate jump height 1.15 m. Input must be released before another jump.
- Default FOV: 90. Slide adds 2.5 degrees, rather than seven. Weapon motion supplies impact without camera shake.
- Casual bots: 52% input multiplier, 1.15 s reaction, 28 m target acquisition range. Regular: 68%, 0.7 s, 38 m. Veteran: 85%, 0.4 s, 52 m. These are initial tuning values, not validated human-equivalent skill ratings.

## Arenas

**Foundry:** 60 × 50 m, previously 44 × 38 m; about 79% more area. Rust, ochre, charcoal, steel ribbing, exhausts, tanks and crane silhouettes. A suspended gantry permits two levels of combat, with a 2.2 m gap to a landing. The firing slit uses separate sill/lintel collision blocks.

**Relay:** 48 × 42 m, previously 32 × 30 m; 110% more area. Cold blue, pale alloy, antenna dishes, server cabinets and distant ice ridges. A central platform, separate balcony and side bridge create elevation choices; the low service gate admits crouching or sliding and blocks standing passage.

These are still compact arenas; they are not open-world levels. Buildings remain deliberate solid cover blocks. Visual paneling does not imply enterable interiors.

## Verification

30 gameplay tests pass, covering all four modes and both maps, movement regressions, weapons, damage, scoring, respawns, team rules, bot navigation and the skill geometry. TypeScript and production build are checked separately. No browser input/visual test or physical low-end-hardware measurement is claimed.

## Remaining v0.2 priorities, in order

1. **Hands-on handling pass:** record input-to-view latency, track a target while strafing, test burst recovery and tune slide exit using player feedback. Test mouse capture, death/selection/re-entry, touch, and focus changes in Chrome and Firefox.
2. **Map playtests:** measure time to first contact, deaths by lane and spawn safety; adjust camping angles and platform advantages. Bots still do not deliberately execute the jump routes.
3. **Animation and collision:** purpose-built articulated arm reloads, break-action shotgun opening, better knee/elbow constraints, capsule collision and ceiling edge cases. Current weapons and ragdolls are procedural approximations.
4. **Device budget:** profile integrated graphics and actual low-end devices, add measured LOD choices and dynamic resolution, and perform repeated-match memory testing. Added geometry and balanced-mode shadows make this measurement necessary.
5. **Online foundation only after handling is settled:** authoritative server simulation, prediction/reconciliation, lag compensation, rooms and reconnect. None of these is implemented by this pass.

The project remains local and unpublished. The previous source-upload approval block is unchanged.
