# v0.5 verification and release

Combat: bounded deterministic recoil, ECHO/KILO/MICA tracer profiles, MICA 16 × 10 pellets / 38 m / delayed falloff, EDGE +6% movement. Hitscan and server authority are unchanged. Hit cues coalesce shotgun pellets.

Match flow: recent-spawn/death penalties, occupancy/cover checks, two-second visible spawn countdown, primary selection, public Quick Play retry and host-controlled private rematch. Private party IDs, tokens and loadouts persist; readiness and life generations reset.

UI: permanent Play / Settings / Locker / Friends navigation; local Noto Sans Bold display font with bundled license; quieter result/respawn panels. Dune has cooler sky contrast and a restrained sun highlight.

Deploy frontend and room server together (protocol 7), using docs/DEPLOY.md. Reload old tabs.

Before v1: test two physical devices over the public deployment, disconnect/rejoin and multiple private rounds; measure frame p95 and RTT on the minimum target device. No claim of production load capacity or subjective sound quality follows from automated tests. Match state remains memory-only and cosmetics device-local. Authentication, persistent economy and multi-instance room routing remain v1 decisions.
